SMP Commands

List of commands used across all plugins

Jobs

Earn money by performing activities related to your profession.

/jobs browse - This allows you to browse the jobs
/jobs join Miner - This would make you join the Miner job
/jobs stats - This shows the stats you have in jobs

Towny

Create towns, claim land and build communities.

/town new MyTown - Creates a new town called MyTown
/town claim - Claim more blocks within a town
/town spawn - Takes you to the towns spawn point
