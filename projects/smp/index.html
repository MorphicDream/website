Details

🌟 Welcome to Enchantra SMP 🌟

Enchantra SMP is a community-focused survival server that expands Minecraft without losing what makes vanilla great.

🏰 Explore a Richer World

Discover dangerous dungeons, hidden treasures, abandoned settlements, and unique structures from the Dungeons & Taverns datapack. Every journey can lead to a new adventure.

⚒️ Slimefun Technology

Unlock hundreds of new items, machines, tools, resources and progression paths through Slimefun and InfinityExpansion.

📈 Level Your Skills

AuraSkills rewards your playstyle with progression in mining, combat, farming, fishing and more.

🏘️ Create Towns

Build a settlement, recruit residents, claim land and grow a thriving community with Towny.

⚰️ Death Without the Frustration

When disaster strikes, your items are protected in a grave, giving you a chance to recover your belongings and get back into the action.

🌎 Player Driven Adventure

Whether you want to become a wealthy merchant, master engineer, fearless explorer, skilled warrior, or the mayor of a bustling town, Enchantra SMP gives you the freedom to create your own story.

Vanilla at heart. Expanded everywhere else!
