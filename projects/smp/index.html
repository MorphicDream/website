Details

🌟 Welcome to Enchantra SMP 🌟 Enchantra SMP is a community-focused survival server that expands Minecraft without losing what makes vanilla great. 🏰 Explore a Richer World Your adventures won't stop at finding a village. With the Dungeons & Taverns datapack, the world is filled with unique structures, dangerous dungeons, hidden treasures, abandoned settlements, and exciting locations waiting to be discovered. 🐉 A Reimagined End Dimension The End has been transformed by the True End datapack, turning it into a full adventure dimension packed with new places to explore, tougher challenges, and reasons to keep returning long after defeating the Ender Dragon. ⚒️ Technology, Magic & Automation Unlock hundreds of new items, machines, tools, resources, and progression paths through Slimefun and its expansions. Build advanced factories, automate tasks, experiment with powerful equipment, and discover entirely new ways to play Minecraft. 📈 Level Up Your Character Gain experience through everyday activities with AuraSkills. Improve your abilities in mining, combat, farming, fishing, enchanting, and more as your character grows stronger over time. 🏘️ Build Nations & Communities Create your own town with Towny, recruit residents, claim land, forge alliances, and build thriving settlements alongside other players. 💰 Earn Rewards While You Play Take on professions through Jobs and earn money for the activities you already enjoy, whether that's mining, building, farming, fishing, or hunting monsters. ⚔️ Custom Challenges & Bosses Encounter unique monsters, special events, and powerful bosses created using MythicMobs, offering rewards and challenges beyond standard vanilla Minecraft. ⚰️ Death Without the Frustration When disaster strikes, your items are protected in a grave, giving you a chance to recover your belongings and get back into the action. 🌎 Player Driven Adventure Whether you want to become a wealthy merchant, master engineer, fearless explorer, skilled warrior, or the mayor of a bustling town, Enchantra SMP gives you the freedom to create your own story. Vanilla at heart. Expanded everywhere else.
