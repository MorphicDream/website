Attis

This is a WIP story of a fictional city called Attis

Overview

Just a fictional story based in a large city called Attis

Platform: Fictional Story

Details

I started writing a story in June 2026. I was just writing for fun but it turned into a bigger story. I then started adding my own twist of magic to it and wanted to avoid AI. I was then told about the website Royal Road and that people often post short stories etc on there. I popped over and had a look and wow it was very cool. So I uploaded my first chapter even though I was super nervous. A posted in the forums that I was nervous and the users all told me it was normal and just to have fun. So I uploaded another chapter and another, and we are as of August the 2nd 2026 at seven chapters uploaded.

I had to make a little teaser for the story (I forget the name) which I will show below.

'Barkle Sintra is a low‑level law officer who wants nothing more than a quiet life. But when an illusionary corpse, a real assassin, and a powerful noble family drag him into their schemes, Barkle’s long‑buried connection to forbidden necrotic magic resurfaces. Now the Urnas family demands his help, a priest hunts the source of the magic, and ancient forces whisper through Barkle’s dreams. The amulet that once ended a war has returned — and Barkle may be the only one who can find it.'

Im not expecting anything from this, but I am enjoying writing it a lot, and hopefully I will be able to finish the story and maybe write other stories within the city of Attis.
